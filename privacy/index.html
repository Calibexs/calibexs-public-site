Privacy.

This page describes the basic privacy practices for Calibexs and its publicly accessible services unless a specific service provides additional information.

Basic request information

Calibexs infrastructure may record technical request information such as IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs, browser or device information, response status, and similar server or security log data.

Cloudflare

Calibexs uses Cloudflare for DNS, network delivery, security, and traffic analytics. Cloudflare may process network and device information while providing those services.

Uploaded content and service data

Some Calibexs services allow users to submit or upload content. Information required to provide that service may be stored or processed, including uploaded files, filenames, technical metadata, generated links, and request information. If a service displays an expiration or retention period, that service-specific information governs how long that content is intended to remain available.

Temporary services

Services designed for temporary sharing should not be treated as permanent storage or backup systems. Users should avoid uploading highly sensitive information unless they understand the risks associated with sharing it.

Security and abuse prevention

Technical information may be reviewed or retained when reasonably necessary to maintain service availability, investigate errors, detect abuse, or protect Calibexs infrastructure and users.

Cookies

Calibexs does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Individual infrastructure or services may use technically necessary security or session mechanisms where required for their normal operation.

External services

Calibexs pages may link to third-party websites and platforms. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and are not governed by this page.

Contact

Privacy questions related to Calibexs can be sent through contact@calibexs.com.
